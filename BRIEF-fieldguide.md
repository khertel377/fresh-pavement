# Fresh Pavement: brief 4, Field Guide (educational layer)

Design reference: open `design/glossary-preview.html` in a browser. Content source: `design/glossary/glossary.json`. Diagrams: `design/glossary/diagrams/*.svg`, generated by `design/glossary/build_preview.py`.

Goal: every term of art in the UI (treatment types, bands, condition/IRI, moratorium, traffic stress levels, bike facility types) becomes a doorway to a short explanation with a diagram and, where we have one, a photo.

Build:
1. Move `glossary.json` and the diagram SVGs into the site (e.g. `public/guide/`). Keep `build_preview.py` as the diagram generator; it's fine to move it under `scripts/`.
2. **One component, three sizes**:
   - **Inline term**: dotted underline, or a small ⓘ for short labels.
   - **Hover card**: only on `(hover:hover)` devices. Shows the diagram + one-liner.
   - **Full entry**: a right sheet on desktop, a bottom sheet on mobile. Shows photo, diagram, rider take, typical band, lifespan, how to spot it and how it's done, plus a photo credit.
3. Wire the terms automatically. Any label rendered from data (legend rows, popup "Last work", band pill, LTS, facility type) looks up `glossary.json` by its id: the same ids as the normalized data (`mill_overlay`, `hipr`, `chip_seal`, …, `lts1`–`lts4`, `protected_lane`, …).
4. Add a **Field Guide** view (a panel link "What do these mean?") with category chips and a grid of entries. Deep links: `#guide/<id>`.
5. **Photos:** download the listed Wikimedia files into the repo (resize to ~960px wide, WebP). Keep the credit + license line visible in the entry, and add the credits to the README. Entries without a photo show the placeholder. HIPR is the priority gap; Kevin will shoot Franklin St.
6. **Accessibility:** terms are real buttons, Esc closes, focus returns to the term, and sheets are `role="dialog"`.
7. Keep the copy editable by non-engineers. Don't hardcode any text from `glossary.json` in components.
